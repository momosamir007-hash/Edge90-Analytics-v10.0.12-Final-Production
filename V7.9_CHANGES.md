# Edge90 Analytics v7.9 — Automated Model Selection Engine

## Goal
Turn Walk-Forward evidence into a conservative, league-specific component policy.

## New
- `ModelSelectionEngine` creates a version-protected policy per league.
- Policy is generated only from chronological Walk-Forward evidence.
- Requires at least 3 valid folds and 30 out-of-sample matches before activation.
- Meta-Learner and Calibration receive separate evidence gates based on their historical activation rates.
- Reliability Engine remains available only when the evidence gate passes.
- Policies are atomically persisted in `models/<league>_selection_v7.9.json`.
- Old or incompatible policy files are rejected.

## Principle
v7.9 is deliberately conservative: insufficient evidence means no automatic claim of superiority.
