# Edge90 Analytics v10.0.11 — Dual-Objective Decision Layer

## Purpose

v10.0.11 follows the 5-league × 5-fold benchmark. It does not add a new
probability model. It makes policy promotion statistically conservative and
separates two legitimate objectives.

## Changes
- Fixed policy promotion to require paired OOS evidence.
- Added exact McNemar test and deterministic paired bootstrap confidence
  intervals for challenger accuracy versus Argmax.
- Added dual objectives:
  - `accuracy_first`: challenger must show statistically significant accuracy
    improvement and pass temporal stability gates.
  - `balanced`: challenger may trade at most 1 percentage point accuracy for
    non-inferior Macro-F1, while remaining temporally stable.
- Argmax remains the immutable production fallback.
- Significance tests operate only on OOS predictions and never alter the
  probability engine.
- Added deterministic seeds and audit diagnostics for reproducibility.

## Safety
No challenger is promoted when temporal evidence is insufficient or when
statistical evidence is weak. A p-value is not treated as proof of future
performance; it is one gate among temporal stability and effect-size checks.
