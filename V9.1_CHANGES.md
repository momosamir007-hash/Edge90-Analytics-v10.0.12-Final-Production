# Edge90 Analytics v9.1 — Explainable AI & Decision Audit Engine

## Overview
v9.1 adds a central explanation and audit layer above the v8.9 lifecycle and v9.0 governance systems.

## Added
- `ExplainableDecisionAuditEngine`.
- Evidence-based explanations for lifecycle and governance decisions.
- Explicit decision, action, reason, confidence and risk fields.
- Human-readable risk-factor explanations.
- Per-league immutable-style history of audit events.
- Version and league compatibility checks.
- Atomic JSON persistence.

## Safety
The audit layer is observational: it does not alter match probabilities, bypass validation gates, force promotions, or suppress rollback actions.

## Storage
`models/decision_audit/<league>/decision_audit_v9.1.json`
