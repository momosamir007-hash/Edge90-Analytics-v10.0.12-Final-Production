# v10.0.10 Benchmark Guide

## Purpose
Run the expensive probability-generation walk-forward once per fold, persist
its output, and then compare all decision policies on the exact same OOS
probabilities. Policy changes therefore cannot alter the probability engine.

## Fast-path rationale
Temporal CV inside `MLPred.train()` is diagnostic for model quality; it does
not choose the fitted pipeline in the current implementation. During the
benchmark runner it is skipped to avoid repeated model fits. Normal production
training keeps `temporal_cv=True` by default.

## Policy metrics
- Accuracy
- Macro-F1
- Draw precision / recall
- Predicted draw rate
- Accuracy standard deviation
- Macro-F1 standard deviation
- Draw-recall standard deviation
- Win rate vs Argmax
- Worst-fold accuracy ratio

## Safety
A challenger is eligible only through `decision_policy_audit.select_policy()`.
Argmax remains the fallback when evidence is insufficient or unstable.

## v10.0.11 note
Benchmark policy promotion should use `decision_layer.py` after OOS predictions
are available. Do not infer statistical significance from aggregate fold means
alone.
