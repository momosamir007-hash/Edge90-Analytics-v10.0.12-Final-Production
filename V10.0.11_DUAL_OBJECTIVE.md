# v10.0.11 Decision Layer

The production decision layer exposes two separately auditable recommendations.

1. **Accuracy-first**: maximize classification accuracy. A challenger must
   outperform Argmax with paired statistical evidence, not merely have a
   higher sample mean.
2. **Balanced**: preserve accuracy within a configurable tolerance while
   seeking Macro-F1 improvement and stable fold behavior.

The two recommendations are never silently conflated. If neither passes its
requirements, Argmax remains the recommendation.

Statistical tests are paired because every policy receives the same OOS
probability vectors. McNemar's exact test evaluates discordant correctness;
a deterministic paired bootstrap supplies an effect-size confidence interval.
